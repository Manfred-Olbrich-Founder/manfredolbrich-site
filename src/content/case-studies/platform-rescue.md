steps:
  label: "What I did"
  heading: "Five moves, in this order."
  items:
    - title: "Secure the foundations"
      text: "Brought every cloud account, database and email service under company control, working with the cloud provider's engineers."
    - title: "Find the right partner"
      text: "Brought in an engineering agency and agreed a sprint plan with a fixed scope and a fixed price."
    - title: "Write it down precisely"
      text: "Rewrote the specifications so a team new to the product could build from them without guessing."
    - title: "Test every release myself"
      text: "Worked through payments, bookings and the whiteboard by hand, and sorted the bugs by urgency."
    - title: "Show it working"
      text: "Demonstrated the finished platform live to the company's investors."
decisions:
  heading: "Four calls I made, and why."
  items:
    - title: "Foundations before features"
      text: "Before a line of new code, I made sure the company owned and could reach everything the product ran on. A new team cannot build on ground you do not control."
    - title: "Fixed scope, fixed price"
      text: "I agreed the full list of work and the cost up front. When the sprint ran about two weeks over, the extra time cost nothing."
    - title: "Take the classroom off the critical path"
      text: "The unreliable classroom could have swallowed the whole sprint. I moved lessons to standard video links as a stopgap, and ran the move from BigBlueButton to the Zoom Video SDK as its own piece of work, with a cost model per lesson."
    - title: "Buy reliability, build the difference"
      text: "Video and transcription are solved problems. I compared providers on cost and quality and bought them in, so engineering time went on what made the product different: the analysis of each lesson."

lessons:
  label: "What I would do differently"
  heading: "Two honest lessons."
  items:
    - title: "Say what is out of scope, twice"
      text: "I treated the classroom as separate work. Not everyone watching the project had heard that clearly, and it caused a delay I could have avoided. I now put “not included” in writing at the start."
    - title: "Ask to see it running"
      text: "Earlier in the project I accepted delivery dates I could not check. Now I ask for working software at every stage, however confident the estimate."
